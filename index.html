About

Designing meaningful digital experiences with creativity and technology.

Hello, I’m Mikyla Zane Limbaga, a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology student passionate about creating meaningful digital experiences. I enjoy combining creativity and technology to design websites and products that are clear, functional, and easy to use.

My strongest interest is UI/UX design, where I turn ideas into intuitive and visually engaging interfaces. I focus on understanding users, organizing information clearly, and creating smooth experiences that look polished while remaining practical and accessible.

I also explore front-end development using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and React. Through every project, I continue to improve my skills, learn new tools, and grow as a designer and developer.
